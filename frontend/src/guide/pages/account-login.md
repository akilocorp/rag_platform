# Sign in

Go to **`/login`**.

## The two fields

**"ITSC Email/Username"** accepts **either** your email address **or** your username —
whichever you remember. You don't need to tell it which one you're typing.

**"Password"** has an eye icon on the right if you want to check what you've typed.

Then press **Login**.

## "Keep me logged in for 30 days"

This checkbox is **ticked by default**, and we'd leave it ticked on your own laptop.

- **Ticked** — you stay signed in for 30 days, across browser restarts.
- **Unticked** — you're signed out as soon as you close the browser. Use this on a shared
  or lab machine.

## Where you land after signing in

| You are | You land on |
|---|---|
| A professor or admin | **`/config_list`** — your assistant list |
| A student | **`/student-dashboard`** — your assignments |
| A student who arrived from a class link | Straight into that class's bot or exercise |
| Anyone with a one-time password | **`/change-password`**, before anything else |

## If it won't let you in

**"Invalid username/email or password"** means one of the two is wrong. Things to try, in
order:

1. Retype the password by hand rather than pasting — a trailing space breaks it.
2. Try your username instead of your email, or the other way round.
3. Use **"Forgot Password?"** on the login page. See
   [Passwords](/userguide/account-password).

> **Not sure whether you ever finished registering?** Signing in works even if you never
> clicked the verification link in your email — you'll just see an "Email not verified"
> banner on your dashboard. So if login is failing, an unverified email isn't the reason.

## Signing out

Click your username in the top-right corner and choose **Logout**.

![The account dropdown, open](/guide-media/account-dropdown.png)

Students find the same menu on their dashboard; professors find it on the assistant list
and every page inside it.
