# Join your class

Your professor will send you a link. It looks like this:

```
https://yoursite.com/join/MGMT5110
```

Open it. That's the whole thing: the link enrolls you and takes you where you need to be.

![The join page](/guide-media/join-page.png)

## If you don't have an account yet

The page shows the class name and two buttons:

- **Create an Account** — the sign-up form, already set to Student and already carrying
  your class code. You don't need to type the code anywhere.
- **I already have an account** — sign in, and you'll be enrolled straight after.

Fill in your **University**, **Email Address**, **Username** and **Password**, then click
**Register**.

Your password needs at least 8 characters including a letter, a number, and a special
character (like `!` or `-`).

## Verify your email

Check your inbox for **"🚀 Welcome to Actr Lab! Please verify your email"** and click the
link inside.

> **The link expires after an hour.** If you're too late, just register again with the same
> details to get a new one.

Can't find it? Check your spam folder.

If you skip this step you can still use the platform — you'll just see an "Email not
verified" reminder on your dashboard.

## Where you end up

Straight into whatever your professor set up. Depending on the class that's a chat
assistant, a video assignment on your dashboard, a simulation lab, or a group exercise
lobby.

If you're already signed in when you open the link, you skip all of the above and land
there immediately.

## Joining more classes

Open each professor's link the same way. You can be in as many classes as you like, and
they'll all appear on your dashboard.

## If something goes wrong

**"Invalid Class Code"** — the link is wrong or has expired. Check you copied the whole
thing, then ask your professor to resend it.

**A sign-in prompt you didn't expect** — normal for most classes. Sign in and you'll
continue automatically.

**An error saying you need to be logged in** — you've been given a direct link to a private
space rather than a class link. Ask your professor for the `/join/...` version.

More in [Troubleshooting](/userguide/student-troubleshooting).

## One thing to know upfront

**The platform is built for a laptop or desktop.** On a phone you'll see a message asking
you to switch devices. This guide is the only part that works on mobile. Plan to do the
actual work on a computer.
