# Create your account

Everyone signs up at **`/register`** — professors and students use the same form, with one
toggle deciding which you get.

![The registration page, with the "I am a" role toggle](/guide-media/register-page.png)

## Step by step

1. Go to **`/register`**, or click **Login** in the top-right and then **"Please Sign Up"**.
2. Under **"I am a"**, choose **Professor** or **Student**.
3. Students also pick a **University** from the searchable dropdown (HKUST, HKU, PolyU,
   HKBU, CUHK). Professors don't see this field.
4. Fill in **Email Address**, **Username**, and **Password**.
5. Click **Register**.
6. Check your inbox for the verification email and click the link inside.

## Choosing a password

Your password must be **at least 8 characters and contain a letter, a number, and a
special character**. The strength meter under the field shows *Weak*, *Medium*, or
*Strong* as you type — aim for Medium or better.

If the form rejects your password, the message is always the same:

> Password must be at least 8 characters with letter, number, and special character.

Nine times out of ten the missing piece is the special character (`!`, `?`, `-`, `#`…).

## Verifying your email

After you register, we send an email titled **"🚀 Welcome to Actr Lab! Please verify your
email"**. Click the link in it and you'll land on a confirmation page that forwards you to
the login screen after a few seconds.

> **The link expires after one hour.** If you click an old one you'll see *"The
> verification link has expired. Please register again."* Register again with the same
> details to get a fresh link.

Didn't get the email? Check your spam folder first — it's the usual culprit.

## Joining a class while you register

If your professor sent you a link that looks like **`yoursite.com/join/MGMT5110`**, open
that link *instead* of going to `/register` directly. You'll see a banner reading
**"🎓 Joining class: MGMT5110"**, the role toggle disappears (a class link always means
student), and you're enrolled in that class the moment your account is created.

Full walkthrough: [Join your class](/userguide/student-join).

## Accounts your admin created for you

If someone set your account up for you, **don't register** — you already have an account.
You'll have been given a one-time password that looks like `wxyz-4821-QRST`. Sign in with
it and you'll be asked to choose your own password straight away. See
[Passwords](/userguide/account-password).
