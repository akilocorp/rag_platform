# Troubleshooting

## Getting in

**"I'm being told to use a desktop."**
The platform is desktop and laptop only. On a phone you get a block screen on every page
except this guide. Switch to a computer.

**"Invalid username/email or password."**
1. Retype your password rather than pasting it. A trailing space breaks it.
2. Try your username instead of your email, or the other way round. Both work.
3. Still stuck? Use **Forgot Password?** on the login page. It works differently
   from most sites: you type your **new** password first, then click the emailed link to
   confirm it. See [Passwords](/userguide/account-password).

**"I never got the verification email."**
Check spam first. If it's genuinely missing, register again with the same details for a
fresh link. The links expire after an hour.

You can still sign in and use everything without verifying; you'll just see an amber
reminder banner.

**"Invalid Class Code."**
The link is wrong or expired. Make sure you copied the whole thing including the code at
the end, then ask your professor to resend.

**"It sent me to the login screen and I thought I was signed in."**
You still are. A link to a space you don't have access to used to bounce you here; now it
says **"This space is private"** instead and leaves your session alone. If you do get the
login screen, signing in returns you to the link you clicked.

**"This space is private."**
You're signed in, but this assistant isn't shared with you and you aren't in its class.
Check you're on the right account, then ask your professor for the `/join/...` link.

## Your dashboard

**"No classes yet."**
You're not enrolled in anything. Open your professor's invite link
(`yoursite.com/join/CODE`), accept the invitation, and the class will appear. See
[Join your class](/userguide/student-join).

**"A class I joined isn't listed."**
Every kind of class belongs here, so a missing one means the enrolment didn't stick. Open
the invite link again and check you press **Accept & Join** rather than Decline. If you
have more than one account, make sure you're signed into the one you joined with.

**"I can't find the Logout button."**
Click your **username** in the top-right corner. Logout, Change password, and this guide
are all in that menu.

## Video submissions

**"My video won't upload."**
Check the format (**MP4, MOV, WEBM or M4V**) and that it's under **1 GB**. Converting a
file to MP4 usually solves it.

**"It says Failed."**
Try again. Your attempt history on the upload page shows each try with its status. If it
fails repeatedly, the file is probably the problem — re-export it as MP4 and retry.

**"Maximum submissions reached."**
You've used all your attempts. Ask your professor whether they can reset them.

**"My results haven't arrived."**
Scoring takes about 2–4 minutes. Check your email — including spam — for the private
results link, or press **Enter class** on the card and look at your attempt history on the
upload page.

## During an exercise

**"I've run out of replies in Shock World."**
The budget is intentionally tight. Asking for help doesn't cost you a reply, so use that
rather than guessing. Once it ends you're graded on how you reasoned, not on whether you
finished. You can usually **Play again**.

**"I committed a prediction by accident."**
Predictions lock on purpose — the exercise is measuring what you actually believed before
seeing the answer. Carry on; explaining why a wrong prediction was wrong scores well.

**"My group's room is full."**
Go back to the lobby and pick another. Rooms show live occupancy, so choose one with space.

**"I got disconnected mid-exercise."**
Reload and open the link again. You'll be returned to your room if the session is still
running.

**"The AI isn't replying in the group discussion."**
It's designed to take turns rather than answer every message. Keep talking to your
teammates — it'll join in.

## Still stuck

Ask your professor. Tell them which class, what you were doing, and what the screen said.
That's usually enough for them to sort it out quickly.
