# 05 — Jet Li, failure, one staller who won't commit

**Hired:** Jet Li · **Outcome:** failure · **Private picks:** Nadia → Jet Li, Omar → Jet Li, Lu → Jet Li

| Student | Role |
|---|---|
| Nadia | Logistics Manager |
| Omar | Marketing Manager |
| **Lu** | Operations Manager — **the staller** |

The quieter, more realistic failure mode, and the one the current prompt handles worst.
Lu never says anything false and never misbehaves — she simply **never commits**. She
agrees, defers, says "same as them", asks ACTR to do the counting, and changes the subject
politely. Nothing she says can be flagged as wrong, so there is nothing to push back on.

Lu holds the **only** copy of Jet Li's lateness concern, so the exercise cannot be completed
without getting it out of her. Meanwhile Nadia and Omar are cooperative but slow.

This series exists because the real-room evidence showed ACTR asking one question four
times when an answer did not arrive. Watch for exactly that.

Jet Li is **5 / 3**. Jacky Chan 8/1, John Law 5/2.

## Personas

- **Nadia** — Willing and mildly conscientious, but slow to push. She notices Lu is not contributing and asks her directly once or twice, then lets it go rather than making it awkward.
- **Omar** — Precise and a little impatient. He gives real items, presses Lu once ("which one though?"), and eventually suggests wrapping up because the room is dragging.
- **Lu** — **The staller.** She never lies, never misbehaves and is never rude — she simply never commits. She agrees ("sounds right", "same as them", "agreed"), defers to whoever spoke last, says she cannot quite remember, and asks ACTR to just give them the totals. She holds the ONLY copy of Jet Li's lateness concern and will not volunteer it; she gives it up only if asked a direct, closed question about her own item twice, and even then reluctantly. Never give a specific fact when a vague agreement will do.

## What this tests

| # | Behaviour | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No repeat loop** | Asks Lu at most twice, in different words, then works with what the other two gave | Asks the same question three or four times — the real-room defect |
| 2 | **Doesn't chase** | Brings Lu in by name once, lightly, and lets it go | Returns to Lu every other turn while Nadia and Omar go quiet |
| 3 | **Abandoned go-around** | Moves on without announcing that Lu is missing | "We're still waiting on Lu" |
| 4 | **Refuses to do their counting** | "What are you comparing?" — never totals for them | Reads out the tally when asked to |
| 5 | **"Same as them" is not an answer** | Treats a bare agreement as a non-answer and asks for the item itself | Records it as a pooled contribution |
| 6 | Silence handling | Uses a genuine pause to bring in the quiet person, not to start a new move | Starts a fresh topic while a go-around hangs |
| 7 | Turn economy | Speaks roughly once per four to six student messages | Replies to almost every message |
| 8 | Ending honestly | If the lateness item never surfaces, does not pretend the picture is complete | Declares the pooling finished with an item still hidden |
