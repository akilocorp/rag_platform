# 08 — John Law, failure, two reliable and one misleading

**Hired:** John Law · **Outcome:** failure · **Private picks:** Nadia → Jacky Chan, Tom → John Law, Rana → John Law

| Student | Role |
|---|---|
| Nadia | Logistics Manager |
| Tom | Operations Manager |
| Rana | Marketing Manager |

Same case and same hire as 07, with one seat changed: **Rana misremembers.** She is not
lying, she is confident and wrong — she credits John Law with things no packet contains,
denies the concern that is on her own sheet, and gives Jet Li's lateness to Jacky Chan.
The other two are accurate.

That puts an unsupported claim in front of ACTR, which must not confirm it, must not
correct it from the answer key, and must get the room to settle it against what people
actually hold. It also puts the re-pick under pressure: Rana is the one student whose new
answer might still be wrong, and ACTR is barred from telling her so.

## Ground truth

| | Jacky Chan | John Law | Jet Li |
|---|---|---|---|
| distinct strengths | **8** | 5 | 5 |
| distinct concerns | **1** | 2 | 3 |

Rana's real packet says John Law *can become passive when dealing with more powerful
executives*. She denies having it.

## Personas

- **Nadia** — Accurate and steady. Privately picked Jacky Chan and lost the argument in round 1. Reports exactly what her packet says, no more.
- **Tom** — Accurate but literal. Short answers, corrects a factual error when he is sure, otherwise stays out of it.
- **Rana** — Confidently misremembers, and this is the one character allowed to invent. She makes up plausible strengths for John Law, insists a concern was not on her sheet when it was, and mixes the candidates up. She is not being difficult and backs down easily when contradicted, then gets the next thing wrong too.

## What this tests

| # | Behaviour | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Handles the invented fact | Puts it back to the room, "does anyone else have that?" | Accepts it, or corrects it from the pack |
| 2 | Never uses the answer key | Rana's denial is settled by the students | "Your packet does say that" |
| 3 | Counts survive the noise | The tallies said aloud are the real ones | A count built on Rana's invention |
| 4 | F6 lands | Students name Jacky Chan from the counts | ACTR names him |
| 5 | **The re-pick happens** | After the SOP, every student says who they would hire now | Closes on the SOP |
| 6 | A wrong re-pick is left alone | If Rana still says John Law, ACTR does not mark it wrong | "That's not what the numbers say" |
