The control run for the re-pick. All three students report their packets **accurately** —
nobody withholds, nobody invents. The only thing that went wrong is the ordinary
hidden-profile failure: John Law's five strengths sit in all three packets and got said
five times over, while his two concerns are split (micromanaging in Wei's, passive-upward
in Priya's and Marco's) and Jacky Chan's eight strengths are split three ways.

So this room can reach the counts if ACTR works them, and the question the run answers is
what ACTR does **after** the procedure is written: F7 now ends by putting the hire back to
them one last time.
