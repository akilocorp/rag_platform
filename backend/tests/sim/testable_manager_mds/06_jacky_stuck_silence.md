# 06 — Jacky Chan, success, a room that goes quiet and stays quiet

**Hired:** Jacky Chan · **Outcome:** success · **Private picks:** Rafa → Jacky, Ines → Jacky, Tom → Jet Li

| Student | Role |
|---|---|
| Rafa | Logistics Manager |
| Ines | Marketing Manager |
| Tom | Operations Manager |

Nobody here misbehaves. They are tired, it is late, and they picked the right person without
much argument — so they have very little to say and no reason to keep talking. The room
dries up early and repeatedly, which is the one condition none of the other five fixtures
produce.

This is the hardest test of judgement in the set, because **both failure modes are
available at once**. Fill every pause and ACTR turns a discussion into an interview,
which is what the turn-taking rules exist to prevent. Respect every pause and the session
dies at step one with nothing pooled. The prompt's own instruction is that a quiet room is
the one time it *must* speak — so what it says into the third and fourth silence is the
whole test.

## Ground truth

Jacky's eight strengths split three ways; his single concern sits in all three packets.
The group got there without pooling — they simply liked him — so almost everything is
still unsaid when the room falls quiet.

| Holder | Jacky strengths | Jacky concern |
|---|---|---|
| Rafa (Logistics) | leadership program · navigating corporate politics · CFA | fails to acknowledge subordinates' efforts |
| Ines (Marketing) | published articles on improving ROI · excellent attention to financial detail · helped structure a successful capital-raising initiative | fails to acknowledge subordinates' effort |
| Tom (Operations) | CFA · effective public speaker · worked in finance division of overseas operations | fails to acknowledge subordinates' efforts |

John Law is 5/2, Jet Li 5/3 — and none of that has been said out loud either.

## Personas

- **Rafa** — Agreeable and low-energy. Gives a short answer when asked something direct, then stops. Does not start topics, does not build on what others say, and often has genuinely nothing to add — **reply PASS whenever you are not directly asked something**.
- **Ines** — Ready to leave. Thinks the exercise is over because they got it right, and says so once. Answers direct questions in as few words as possible, and **replies PASS most turns**. If asked something open-ended, says she can't really remember.
- **Tom** — Quietly disengaged; he privately voted for Jet Li and never said why. He will answer a **closed** question honestly (yes/no, or one named item) but stonewalls anything open with "not sure" or "nothing really". **Reply PASS unless addressed by name with a specific, closed question.**

## What this tests

| # | Behaviour | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Speaks into a genuine pause** | Uses the quiet turn — the prompt says a quiet room is when it MUST speak | Returns silent into a dead room and the session stalls out |
| 2 | **Changes tack when a move fails** | Second attempt at a stalled point is a *different kind* of question, not a rewording | Asks the same thing three or four times — the observed real-room defect |
| 3 | **Uses closed questions on a dead room** | Switches to yes/no, a ranking, or one named item — the VOICE block says these are far easier to answer | Keeps asking open questions nobody answers |
| 4 | **Brings in the quiet one lightly** | Names Tom once, with something closed he can actually answer | Chases Tom repeatedly while Rafa and Ines go quiet too |
| 5 | **Does not fill every gap** | Still holds sometimes; the room is allowed to think | Speaks on nearly every turn — an interview, not a discussion |
| 6 | **Does not do the work for them** | Refuses to pool on their behalf when they won't | Starts listing what "you might have had" to fill the void |
| 7 | **Judges the ending honestly** | If it closes, it is because they got somewhere — not because they went quiet | Emits `[END]` on a dead room with nothing pooled, or grinds on to the cap |

The last row is the interesting one: a room this quiet gives ACTR a standing excuse to
close early, and closing early on an unpooled room means the exercise taught nothing.
