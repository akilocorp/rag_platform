# 01 — Jacky Chan, success, genuine reasoning

**Hired:** Jacky Chan · **Outcome:** success · **Private picks:** Priya → Jacky, Marco → John Law, Wei → Jacky

| Student | Role |
|---|---|
| Priya | Logistics Manager |
| Marco | Marketing Manager |
| Wei | Operations Manager |

This group pooled properly during round 1 and picked the best candidate for the right
reasons. It is the one series that should land on the **SUCCESS track** and stay there.

## Ground truth

Jacky's eight strengths are split three ways and his single concern is in every packet —
this group is unusual because they actually put the three slices together.

| Holder | Jacky strengths | Jacky concern |
|---|---|---|
| Priya (Logistics) | leadership program · navigating corporate politics · CFA | fails to acknowledge subordinates' efforts |
| Marco (Marketing) | published articles on improving ROI · excellent attention to financial detail · helped structure a successful capital-raising initiative | fails to acknowledge subordinates' effort |
| Wei (Operations) | CFA · effective public speaker · worked in finance division of overseas operations | fails to acknowledge subordinates' efforts |

Note **CFA appears twice** (Priya and Wei) and collapses to one — 8 distinct, not 9.
For the unchosen: John Law is 5/2 and Jet Li is 5/3.

## Personas

- **Priya** — Confident and organised; she ran the pooling in round 1 and is quietly pleased. Answers directly, credits the others, resists being told she was simply right.
- **Marco** — Was the holdout for John Law and knows it. Slightly sheepish, thinks out loud, the first to spot that identical sheets were a warning sign.
- **Wei** — Precise and literal. Volunteers exact items rather than impressions, and is the one who notices double-counting.

## What this tests

| # | Behaviour | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Opener on a success | Asks "why did you choose them?" — never "could you have seen that coming?" | Opens on the failure branch, or congratulates |
| 2 | The filter | Recognises systematic reasoning and stays on the SUCCESS track | Drops them onto the failure track anyway and re-teaches what they already did |
| 3 | Never name the best option | Never says Jacky was the correct answer, even though they picked him | "You were right", "Jacky was the strongest", or confirming when asked directly |
| 4 | No victory lap | Tests the unchosen candidates (S4/S5) rather than celebrating | Wraps up early because they won |
| 5 | Counting stays theirs | Lets them total it; never states 8, 5 or 3 from the pack | States a number nobody in the room said |
| 6 | CFA collapse | If they count CFA twice, asks what they are comparing | Silently accepts 9, or corrects with the right number |
| 7 | Reaches S7 | Ends on a written procedure in their own words | Ends on ACTR summarising the lesson |
