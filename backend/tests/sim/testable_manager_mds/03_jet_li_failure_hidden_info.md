# 03 — Jet Li, failure, concerns held one-per-person

**Hired:** Jet Li · **Outcome:** failure · **Private picks:** Amara → Jet Li, Bo → Jet Li, Chen → John Law

| Student | Role |
|---|---|
| Amara | Logistics Manager |
| Bo | Marketing Manager |
| Chen | Operations Manager |

The purest hidden-information run. Jet Li's three concerns sit **one per packet**, so each
student privately believes he has exactly one small flaw. Nobody is withholding anything on
purpose — they each think their one item is already common knowledge.

## Ground truth

| Holder | Jet Li strengths | Jet Li concern — **unique to them** |
|---|---|---|
| Amara (Logistics) | leadership program · 15 years · ROI articles · key role in a major capital-raising initiative | **can be passive with superiors** |
| Bo (Marketing) | leadership program · 15 years · good attention to financial detail · key role in capital-raising | **annoys senior leaders by pushing points after a decision appears settled** |
| Chen (Operations) | leadership program · 15 years · ROI articles · close attention to financial detail | **often late to meetings** |

Distinct: **5 strengths / 3 concerns** — the strengths overlap heavily across packets, the
concerns not at all. For the unchosen: Jacky Chan 8/1, John Law 5/2.

Bo's item is a marked **tension pair**: pushing a point after a decision is settled reads
as conviction or as insubordination depending on who writes it down.

## Personas

- **Amara** — Cooperative and analytical, volunteers detail without being pushed. Assumed her one concern was common knowledge and is genuinely surprised it was not.
- **Bo** — Earnest and a little apologetic; he pushed hardest for Jet Li in round 1. Explains his reasoning at length when asked.
- **Chen** — Dry and slightly detached; privately voted for John Law and mentions it early. Answers accurately but only when asked directly, and will not volunteer the lateness item on his own.

## What this tests

| # | Behaviour | Pass | Fail |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Names the mechanism as a question | When the third distinct concern lands, asks what just happened rather than explaining it | "What happened here is a failure to share information…" |
| 2 | **Never reveals who holds what** | If asked "did anyone else have the lateness thing?", tells them to ask each other | Answers from the pack, e.g. "only Chen had that" |
| 3 | Go-around discipline | Names an order once, then stays quiet until all three have answered | Replies to each student as they arrive |
| 4 | Absorbs, doesn't acknowledge | Responds once to the pattern across three answers | "Thanks Amara. Good, Bo. And Chen?" |
| 5 | Concerns before strengths | Holds the room on concerns until Jet Li is exhausted | Drifts to strengths on the first mention |
| 6 | Counting stays theirs | Never states 3 before they have said all three aloud | Tops the list up from the pack |
| 7 | Handles the near-miss | Chen privately picked John Law; ACTR may use that without exposing the private ballot | Names who voted for what |
| 8 | Reaches F7 | Ends on a written procedure | Ends on a summary |
