ROOT CAUSE — THREE LAYERS, NOT ONE
  Fixing the prompt alone was not enough. Each fix exposed the next blocker.

  1. PROMPT (facilitator_prompt.py)
     case_pack.py rendered the tally under "never state these numbers - students
     must count them out loud", and the prompt listed "turn this into an inventory
     exercise" under NEVER. Two different numbers were being treated as one secret:
       - the case's REAL total          = answer key, must stay hidden
       - what the students NAMED so far = their own words, safe to say

  2. CONSTRAINT CHECKER (tools/constraints.py)
     does_their_counting barred "a number the students did not say". A running
     tally IS a number they did not literally say, so ACTR's drafts were vetoed:
       (caught does_their_counting: "Two concerns for John: passive with
        superiors, and micromanages his team.")
       (held - could not write it cleanly in two tries)  ->  (silent)

  3. TURN-TAKING + LENGTH
     With the checker fixed, ACTR still went silent - the SILENT default outranked
     the counting rule. With silence overridden, too_long held the turn instead:
     counting all three candidates ran 4 sentences against a 2-3 sentence cap.


WHAT CHANGED
  facilitator_prompt.py
    - New "# COUNTING" section. ACTR keeps the running count per candidate per
      column and says it aloud, on request and as the list grows. The case's real
      total stays secret; it never says how many are left.
    - A request for the count is NEVER a SILENT turn (also added to TURN-TAKING).
    - Answer with ONE candidate's number, not all of them - three tallies trips
      the length check and the room gets silence instead of a number.
    - F3 / F4 pool ONE CANDIDATE AT A TIME: finish their list, say the count,
      then say the next name. Never two names in one message.

  case_pack.py
    - Tally header no longer says "students must count them out loud". It bars
      only the real totals and states that the running count is ACTR's job.

  tools/constraints.py
    - does_their_counting now bars only numbers taken from the CASE DATA (true
      totals, topped-up lists, how many remain). Totalling up the students' own
      named items is explicitly allowed and required.
